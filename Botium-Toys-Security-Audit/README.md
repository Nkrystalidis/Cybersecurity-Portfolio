# Internal Security Audit & Risk Assessment (Botium Toys)

## Scenario
Botium Toys is a small U.S. business experiencing rapid online growth. With an expanding worldwide customer base, the IT department faces increasing pressure to support the online market securely. The IT Manager initiated an internal audit to maintain compliance, ensure business continuity, and identify vulnerabilities within the company's infrastructure.

## Task
The objective was to conduct an internal security audit using the National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF). This required reviewing the current scope, goals, and risk assessment report to evaluate the organization's existing security posture and identify missing controls.

## Action
- Analyzed the IT department's current asset inventory and risk assessment report.
- Evaluated existing physical, technical, and administrative controls (e.g., CCTV, Firewalls, Antivirus, Password Policies).
- Completed a comprehensive Controls and Compliance Checklist, assessing adherence to industry standards including:
  - **PCI DSS** (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)
  - **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation)
  - **SOC** (System and Organizations Controls)

## Outcome & Recommendations
Based on the audit, the current risk score is 8/10. To mitigate these risks and improve the security posture, I recommend implementing the following critical controls:
1. **Identity & Access Management (IAM):** Implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), enforce Least Privilege, and deploy a centralized password management system.
2. **Data Protection:** Implement data encryption for all internal databases storing credit card information and PII.
3. **Infrastructure Security:** Install an Intrusion Detection System (IDS) and establish a formal Disaster Recovery Plan with automated data backups.
